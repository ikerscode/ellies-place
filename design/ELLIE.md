# Ellie: personal facts and tone

This file is the only source for anything personal. Never invent facts about her beyond what is written here.

## Who the game is for

Ellie. She played and loved the first game, *Every Version of Me* (`reference/every-version-of-me.html`), on her MacBook. She likes The Sims, Pokémon and video games in general. This game is a gift from someone who cares about her. It should feel made for her, and it should be fun.

## Her look (the player character)

- Black hair with green streaks at the front and on both sides.
- Nose ring.
- Tattoos on her arms and shoulders, so default outfits show her shoulders and arms.
- Dark, moody, cute style.
- Her sprite is the original design from game 1 (`E_DOWN`, `E_UP`, `E_SIDE` in the reference). Keep that design and extend it with new animations. Do not make her resemble any existing character from any show or game.

## Tone rules (hard)

1. Playful, cosy and funny. This is a home, not a love letter.
2. Agents must not write romantic messages, confessions or love letters, and must not reuse the letters or hearts text from game 1.
3. The person giving the gift does not appear as a character.
4. A personal mail slot exists in `src/content/personal.ts`, empty by default, for the giver to fill himself.
5. "Grazie per essere te stessa" may appear only as an optional framed-print decor item, enabled by a flag in `personal.ts` (default: on).
6. Never mention her surname, location or anything not on this page.
